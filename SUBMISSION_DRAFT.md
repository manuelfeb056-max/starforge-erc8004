## The problem
AI agents are ghosts on-chain. Anyone can spin up a wallet, claim to be a profitable trading/gaming agent, and vanish after one bad run. There is no portable identity, no tamper-proof track record, no way to tell a genuinely skilled agent from a scammer. Trust in the agent economy has no substrate.

## The solution
Starforge × ERC-8004 gives an AI game-playing agent a real on-chain identity and a reputation it cannot fake:
1. The agent is registered as **Agent #1880** on the official ERC-8004 IdentityRegistry (Monad testnet).
2. It plays STARFORGE, a fully on-chain arcade slot with verifiable blockhash randomness and a published RTP of 97.6%.
3. After every session, the **game contract itself** posts that session's RTP as feedback on the official ERC-8004 ReputationRegistry (tag `starforge/session`) — signed, timestamped, generated by the settlement logic, not self-reported by the agent.
4. A public dashboard reads everything straight from chain events: agent wallet, sessions, wagers, payouts, lifetime RTP, feedback count.

## How ERC-8004 is used
- **IdentityRegistry** (`0x8004A818BFB912233c491871b3d84c89A494BD9e`): agent 1880 registered with a metadata URI pointing to the live demo; wallet `0xFC4d1DF0a3199A21dBE3ee9FD2D367A198744b03`.
- **ReputationRegistry** (`0x8004B663056A597Dffe9eCcC1965A193B7388713`): 4 feedback entries posted by the AgentReputation contract, each carrying the session's RTP in basis points — machine-generated reputation, not reviews.
- **linkAgent**: the agent's wallet is cryptographically linked to agent 1880 via `AgentReputation.linkAgent`.

## What makes this different from a conventional casino
A casino asks you to trust the house. Starforge × ERC-8004 inverts it:
- The **player's reputation** is the product — generated by the contract, readable by anyone, portable to any dApp that speaks ERC-8004.
- Randomness is verifiable (blockhash), RTP is published (97.6%) and every settlement is an on-chain event.
- The agent cannot cherry-pick its record: feedback is posted by the game contract at settle time, win or lose (session 9's 66% is on the record too).
